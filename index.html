1 · Opener

Greeting"Hi [Name], this is Michael Tettey from Spero. How's it going today?"

(Let them respond)

Frame + confirm identity"I know you weren't expecting my call, so I'll be brief. I found you on LinkedIn as the [Position] at [Company] — is that still correct?"

2 · Discovery Question

"Just out of curiosity, how are you guys currently generating most of your leads — is it mainly inbound, outbound, referrals, or a mix?"

(Listen to their answer)

"Do you have an internal team booking meetings for you, or reps making outbound calls?"

"And when it comes to outbound prospecting, is filling your AEs' pipelines with qualified meetings something that's important for you right now?"

3 · The Branch: Which Pitch Do You Use?

Their answer to "do you have an internal team" decides which pitch comes next. Don't mix them — each is built for a different situation.

They already have an internal SDR / outbound team →

Use Pitch A — Test Drive. The $4/hr "try before you commit" framing only makes sense when there's something to compare it against.

They have no internal team / no one doing outbound →

Use Pitch B — Core SDR. Skip the trial language — pitch the outcome: pipeline, qualified meetings, cost-effectiveness.

4 · Pitch A — Test Drive (has a team)

"The reason I'm reaching out is we just launched a Talent Test Drive Program. It gives companies access to trained remote SDR talent from Latin America for as little as $4 an hour during the first two months, so you can see if they're a good fit before making any long-term commitment. Like me, I'm actually calling you from Argentina right now. We handle the recruiting and training — you just manage them like any other remote member of your team. Does that sound relevant to you?"

5 · Pitch B — Core SDR Program (no team)

"We help companies like yours prospect into bigger accounts by filling your pipeline with quality appointments in a cost-effective way with our SDR program."

Value Props

  • Fills your AE's pipeline with qualified, pre-scheduled appointments
  • Prospects match your ICP (Ideal Client Profile) — ready for a demo, not cold names
  • Extremely cost-effective vs. hiring/training in-house

"What we do at Spero is help companies build outbound pipeline by providing trained remote SDRs who prospect, qualify, and book meetings with your ideal customers — in a much more cost-effective way than hiring an equivalent SDR in the U.S."

6 · The Close (Micro-Commitment)

"Actually, the reason I called wasn't to try to sell you anything over the phone. I just want to see if it makes sense to have a quick 15-minute conversation with my Account Executive. He can learn more about your sales process, ICP, and current goals, and show you exactly how we've helped companies like yours generate more qualified meetings. Would you be open to a quick conversation tomorrow, or would Thursday work better?"

7 · Confirming the Meeting

"Tell you what — I'll send you an invite for [Day] at [Time]. If anything comes up, you can just hit reschedule. That way, we at least have a placeholder. Is that fair? Could you confirm if the email I have is correct? I will send the link to your email right away."

8 · Objection Handling

"Send me an email."

"Sure, I can send an email, but the reason I'm calling is just to set up a quick 15-minute chat to see if we'd be a good fit for your company, no strings attached. Just so I don't bug you in the future — I'm just asking you for 20 minutes with our AE, where he shows you exactly how it runs in a company like yours. No pitch, just a look. Is it worth it? Would [Day] at [Time] work better?"

"I'm not interested."

"Totally fair. Is that because of timing, or is your team genuinely not looking to add support right now? Perhaps it would be helpful if I sent you an email with information about what we offer? I could follow up with a call next week to see if you've had a chance to read it and answer any questions."

"We already have SDRs / We outsource already."

"That's great to hear! Many founders we talk to are still stretched thin managing multiple responsibilities internally. Curious if there's an area where extra support could help your team at a fraction of the cost?"

9 · Alternative Pitch: The Reverse Pitch

Use if the prospect seems busy and you need a fast, punchy hook.

"Hey, this is Michael Tettey with Spero. I'm talking with a lot of sales leaders right now, and I'm seeing two challenges come up pretty consistently. One: They have sales reps spending too much time prospecting instead of selling. Two: They're struggling to create a consistent flow of qualified meetings. I'm curious — is either of those something you're dealing with right now?"

(If yes) "That's exactly where we help. Spero provides trained SDRs who handle top-of-funnel prospecting so your team can focus on closing. Would it be crazy to spend 15 minutes comparing how you're handling that today with what we're doing for other companies?"

10 · Gatekeeper Script

Gatekeeper: "Hi, who's calling?"

You: "Hi, this is Michael Tettey with Spero. I'm trying to reach [Prospect's Name]. Is he/she available?"

Gatekeeper: "What is this regarding?"

You: "Sure. We just launched a program that lets companies test trained remote SDR talent from Latin America before making a long-term hiring commitment. I just have a quick question for [Prospect's Name] to see if this could be relevant for the team."

Gatekeeper: "Can you send an email?"

You: "Absolutely, I can do that. Before I do, is [Prospect's Name] the person who handles sales development or outbound prospecting? (Wait for yes) Perfect. And what's the best way to reach them directly? Is there a better time to catch them?"

17 · SDR & Pipeline Glossary

Some prospects will genuinely ask "what's a CRM?" or "what's pipeline?" — know these cold.

What an SDR does

  • Prospects: finds potential customers via research, calls, emails, LinkedIn
  • Qualifies leads: checks fit on need, budget, authority, timing
  • Starts conversations: opens doors, generates interest
  • Sets meetings: main KPI is booking qualified appointments for the AEs

What an SDR doesn't do

  • Doesn't close deals
  • Usually doesn't run demos (company-dependent)
  • Doesn't manage accounts

Why SDRs matter

  • Keeps the top of funnel full
  • Frees AEs to focus on closing, not hunting
  • Creates predictable, repeatable pipeline

What is pipeline?

All the deals someone is working on, plus their dollar value, plus where they sit in the sales cycle — qualified prospects, scheduled meetings, opportunities created, deals in negotiation. In plain terms: future money you haven't earned yet but are working toward. Weak pipeline usually means missed quota down the line.

The Spero package

Monthly subscription: fully dedicated SDR(s), a Client Success Manager, analytics reporting, GoHighLevel licenses, and a lead list (via Seamless.ai partnership). SDRs are based worldwide; Spero HQ is in Lehi, Utah.
